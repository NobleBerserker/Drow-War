Greetings of Night and Fire!

Since you clearly hanker to visit the upper world before the  
rest of us, my nephew, then by all means do so. You have my  
blessing to visit the human settlements and ensure that none  
is forewarned of our little friends’ arrival. Deal with rumours  
in the usual way – laughter is much more effective than a slit  
throat.

Be especially sure to pass any pertinent information along  
to our agents in Saragost. Be diplomatic about it, damn you – they have been in service longer than yourself and are not  
likely to appreciate a smarmy brat parading his expertise  
about. We are all of House Arakh, remember, from the highest  
to the lowest and respect is our watchword.

Most importantly, ensure that the Circle has not been  
interfered with, if you can. I have had dreams – troubling  
dreams, which cannot be dismissed as so many dust-visions.  
There is nothing in that part of the world but rocks, fish and  
inbred peasants, so our course should be clear. Yet I wonder.  
I wonder.

Send word to me often. Fail, and I disown you. It is only what  
you would expect.

Your affectionate Uncle,  
**A.**
