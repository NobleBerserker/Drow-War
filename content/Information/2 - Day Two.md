The time has come to reclaim [[Starkweather's Sword]], and it took some convincing but we did. As I held it into my hands, I felt a connection to it. It was mine to wield.

The time had come to finally head down the mine, towards the weird door. As we opened the door to check inside an invisible duergar attacked us, it was tough fighting someone we could not see, but in the end we defeated him. As we searched his things we found a [[Duergar Letter]]. We had to warn everyone! The Duergar and the Drow were planning an attack in 10 days!

We warned Bronce, while some were reluctant to leave, I managed to convince them. The [[Mayor Goodchild|Mayor]] told us to warn [[Saragost]] and he could provide us with horses.

We took some time to plan ahead, and we decided we would take the path to [[Lichgate]]. 
As we were getting to [[Lichgate]] we fought a [[Angkheg]] a gigantic insect-like beast. As we saved the villagers we saw a [[Halfling]] rushing towards us, it was constable [[Apple Merrywind]] who would join us in our adventure. On the monster we found another black arrow from a previous fight.

The Council of [[Lichgate]] seemed to enjoy conversing and were not taking the threat seriously, we had to earn their trust. There was another monster that was stealing sheep! By the description it sounded like a [[Choker]]. 

In the end we had to defeat two chokers and that finally earned the councils trust. We finally convinced them to leave for [[Saragost]] while they would send people to warn the villages.
