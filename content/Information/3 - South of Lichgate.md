A few days into our journey, we traveled south toward **[[Lower Scumsgrove]]**.  
On the road leading into town, we were **ambushed by a pack of ghouls**, which we successfully defeated.

We took shelter at the local inn, **Trypia Bota**, where we spoke with the innkeeper. He shared unsettling rumors: the _traders_ who govern [[Lower Scumsgrove]] are said to be **[[vampires]]**. By my knowledge, [[vampires]] are vulnerable to sunlight, return to their tombs when destroyed, can drain life (causing _negative levels_), and command creatures of the night. While likely hearsay, the atmosphere here makes such claims hard to dismiss.

The town's leadership is clearly compromised. **[[Mayor Hopkis]]** obeys the traders without question, and the **[[Captain of Scumsgrove|captain of the guard]]**—under the guild’s thumb—is notorious for cruelty, including the torture and killing of prisoners. There are whispers of a **hidden passage** leading to the town hall. The presence of **slavery** is widespread and disturbingly open.

We ensured to speak with all the key figures: the [[Mayor Hopkis|mayor]], the [[Captain of Scumsgrove|captain]], and ultimately the trader **[[Velmira]]**, who appears to be the true authority in the town.

From there, we journeyed on to **[[Churney-on-the-Green]]**, following the guidance of **[[Apple Merrywind]]**. We were instructed to seek out a [[Churney druid|druid]]. The **[[Churney druid|druid]]** we found pledged to protect her homeland. She welcomed us to rest in her **grove** and offered to craft magical items for us.


![[Dogfight.png|300]]


We encountered **Barnabas Jeso**, who was under attack by several **goblins** and an **ogre**. We dispatched them decisively, but his friend **Filbert** was killed in the fight. **[[Rey]]** informed Barnabas about the approaching war.

We then arrived at **Hocton Burrow**, where a **ranged tournament** was underway.  
**Captain Lucy Yavril** was the key contact to persuade Hocton Burrow to begin preparing for the war.

We participated in the tournament. **Rein Sin** lost in the drinking contest but performed well overall, ultimately winning the wrestling match.  
I qualified for the ranged competition but was eliminated.

The tournament’s winner seized the trophy bow and snapped it in half — an act clearly meant to demoralize the people. We followed him, suspecting foul intentions, and soon realized he was preparing to attack us. We fought him, and I decapitated him. He was a **[[drow]]**, carrying a **[[Drow Letter|letter]]**.
