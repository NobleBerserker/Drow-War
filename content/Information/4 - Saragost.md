As we approached **Saragost**, we found a crowd gathered at the gates — a long queue of travelers waiting to enter. Just as we resigned ourselves to the wait, a **blue-caped knight** appeared and offered to escort us inside.

However, it was a trap. He led us straight into an **ambush**, where two **drow archers** opened fire on us. I was struck by an arrow coated in **drow poison** and quickly succumbed to sleep.

![[SaragostKnight.png|300]]

As I lay unconscious during the fight, I experienced a vision: **Saragost** was stricken by disease, the **city gates** lay in ruin from some unseen calamity, and **armies** were marching toward it. Above, **acid rain** loomed, threatening to fall.

After the battle, we split the party. **Rein** and I went to speak with the **council**, while **Phoenix** and **Rey** remained behind.

The council was composed of:

- **[[Sir Jeren Falconio]]**
    
- **[[Michael Dunsany]]**
    
- **[[Count Thaddeus Crastic]]**
    
- **[[Lady Arigane Zakawe]]**
    
- **[[Grandlord Peregrine Yomah]]**
    

I informed them of everything: the letters, the plans to attack, and the traitors like the knight who had led us into the trap. They were convinced by my testimony, and we had already **tied up the traitor**.

Meanwhile, **Phoenix** and **Rey** encountered some trouble with the guards. However, they cooperated and handled the situation well, clearing things up quickly. The **traitor** was judged accordingly.

Later, we visited **Michael Dunsany**, who revealed something extraordinary: we are **Starborn**, chosen by the light of a star that has entered our bodies. In opposition, the enemy commands **dark champions** known as the **Host**, each of whom carries the darkness within them.

The city guards stopped our party as we made our way through the streets. After explaining our purpose — that we needed to reach the graveyard — they reluctantly agreed to accompany us. The march was long and uneasy, but at last we stood before the tomb of Yarnak. Inside, we returned the token, and to our unease discovered a hairy skeleton laid to rest within.

The wizard Thuz referred to Yarnak dismissively as _“his dog”_ — a remark that made us wonder about their history.

Later, in the council chambers, we learned of a hidden door. Beyond it lay the **Storm Dreamer’s Stone**, guarded by strong wards and protections.

I confided in [[Lady Arigane Zakawe]] about the strange visions that had plagued me, hoping for insight. Meanwhile, Rey buried himself in study at the library, while Phoenix turned his talents to espionage. He uncovered a secret meeting between the count and the guard captain. The truth struck like a dagger: the [[Count Thaddeus Crastic|count]] was a traitor. Silence cloaked the chamber as Phoenix cast his spell, aiding the [[Sir Jeren Falconio|captain]] as a drow assassin struck. Together, they overcame both the drow and the treacherous count.

After some time spent purchasing supplies and equipment, we turned our attention to a more humble threat: rats. The swarms were relentless, and in the chaos we discovered two children. To our horror, they revealed themselves as [[vampires]] — [[Bahn and Lisbet]]. After a fierce battle, they dissolved into smoke, fleeing to their lair. We followed the trail to a cellar, where a vampire lady came to investigate. I entranced her with words while my companions dealt with the children. In the end, they were staked and carried into the sunlight. I voiced my disapproval, but the deed was already done. Though the true hand behind the rat attack remains unknown, at least the threat has been ended.

At last, we journeyed to Astercote. But where we expected to find a village, we found only a vast crater yawning in the earth.
