As we approached **Saragost**, we found a crowd gathered at the gates — a long queue of travelers waiting to enter. Just as we resigned ourselves to the wait, a **blue-caped knight** appeared and offered to escort us inside.

However, it was a trap. He led us straight into an **ambush**, where two **drow archers** opened fire on us. I was struck by an arrow coated in **drow poison** and quickly succumbed to sleep.

![[SaragostKnight.png|300]]

As I lay unconscious during the fight, I experienced a vision: **Saragost** was stricken by disease, the **city gates** lay in ruin from some unseen calamity, and **armies** were marching toward it. Above, **acid rain** loomed, threatening to fall.

After the battle, we split the party. **Rein** and I went to speak with the **council**, while **Phoenix** and **Rey** remained behind.

The council was composed of:

- **[[Sir Jeren Falconio]]**
    
- **[[Michael Dunsany]]**
    
- **[[Count Thaddeus Crastic]]**
    
- **[[Lady Arigane Zakawe]]**
    
- **[[Grandlord Peregrine Yomah]]**
    

I informed them of everything: the letters, the plans to attack, and the traitors like the knight who had led us into the trap. They were convinced by my testimony, and we had already **tied up the traitor**.

Meanwhile, **Phoenix** and **Rey** encountered some trouble with the guards. However, they cooperated and handled the situation well, clearing things up quickly. The **traitor** was judged accordingly.

Later, we visited **Michael Dunsany**, who revealed something extraordinary: we are **Starborn**, chosen by the light of a star that has entered our bodies. In opposition, the enemy commands **dark champions** known as the **Host**, each of whom carries the darkness within them.
